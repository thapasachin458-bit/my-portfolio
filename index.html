Who I Am

Where Code Meets Iron

I'm Sachin Thapa — a bachelor's degree student with a passion for information technology and an unwavering commitment to competitive powerlifting. To me, both fields are driven by the same principle: progressive overload. In the gym, that means adding weight to the bar — I've built my total to 602.5 kg (232.5 squat / 125 bench / 245 deadlift). In tech, it means constantly leveling up my skills, tackling harder problems, and shipping better solutions.

I've completed 9–10 academic IT projects across networking, web development, databases, and more. Outside the screen, I compete in powerlifting — stepping on the platform for the first time in 2024 and earning a podium finish in all 4 competitions entered so far. That 100% podium rate isn't luck — it's the result of structure, preparation, and refusing to leave anything on the table.

"The same mindset that keeps me under the bar when it gets heavy is what keeps me debugging at 2 AM. Discipline isn't a switch — it's a lifestyle."
